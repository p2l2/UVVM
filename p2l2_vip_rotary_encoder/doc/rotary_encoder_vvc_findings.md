| F-002 | 2026-10-21 | Phase 2 – Verification requirements (spec change rev 0.4) | Angle movement can be added on top of the accepted D-1 timing (first edge `t_edge` after the start, at the edge boundary) | With the position sitting on an edge boundary, a reverse move from rest emits its first edge immediately and a forward move emits it after `t_edge`.
